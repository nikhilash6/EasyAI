Licences for the models EasyAI Setup downloads from its own mirror
==================================================================

Some models sit behind a sign-in at their original home. EasyAI Setup hosts
copies of those so you do not need to create accounts just to get started.

Redistributing them carries conditions, and passing these licences on to you is
one of them. That is why this folder exists.

  LICENSE-LTX-2.x.md
      Covers the LTX 2.5 files used by the Video workflows:
        ltx-2.5-22b-distilled-transformer-comfy-int8-convrot.safetensors
        ltx-2.5-video-vae-bf16.safetensors
        ltx-2.5-audio-vae-bf16.safetensors
        gemma4-12b-with-proj-ltx-2.5-comfy-int8-convrot.safetensors
      Original: https://huggingface.co/Lightricks/LTX-2.5
      Section 3 permits redistribution; Section 3.2 requires that you receive
      this copy of the Agreement. Section 4 and Attachment A list use-based
      restrictions that apply to you as well.
      Note: organisations with revenue of $10,000,000 or more per year need a
      paid licence from Lightricks for any non-personal use.

  KNP_000003000.safetensors  (Krea 2 NSFW V4, a LoRA for the Image workflows)
      Original: https://civitai.com/models/2725430
      Published on Civitai allowing derivatives, re-licensing and use without
      credit. Check the model page for the current terms before redistributing
      it further.

Everything else EasyAI Setup installs comes straight from its original home -
HuggingFace or Civitai - under whatever licence that source states. This folder
covers only the mirrored copies.
